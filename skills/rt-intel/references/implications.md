# Implications for planning

The Threat Profile Brief ends with what the selected actor and its techniques imply for Phase 3 (`rt-emulate`). This is the handoff: it develops the **how** of the approach without reopening the signed objectives. Record it in `implications_for_planning`:

- **initial_access_approach**: the initial access the actor's tradecraft implies for these objectives.
- **infrastructure_implied**: tooling and infrastructure the approach implies. Feeds the planning pack Part B (infrastructure and teardown).
- **specialist_capability_implied**: any specialist capability the approach needs. Feeds Part C procurement, so flag long lead-time items now.
- **opsec_constraints**: the OPSEC constraints the engagement must observe, consistent with the actor's `opsec_posture`.

Keep each grounded in the actor and techniques already recorded in this brief. These are implications of the intelligence, not a plan; `rt-emulate` builds the attack path per objective from them, and every step it plans traces back to a technique in `#threat-profile` or is justified from the actor's documented tradecraft.
